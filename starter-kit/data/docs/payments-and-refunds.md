# Payments and Refunds

- **Doc ID:** BG-POL-05
- **Version:** 1
- **Effective:** 1 March 2024

## 1. When to pay

Residential jobs are paid on completion. We send the invoice by email and text on the same day. Commercial clients with an approved account have 14-day payment terms.

## 2. How to pay

We accept card on the day (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), bank transfer and Apple/Google Pay. Card payments have no surcharge. We do not accept cash or cheques.

## 3. Late payment

Invoices unpaid after 30 days get a reminder. After 60 days we may refer the debt to a collection agency.

## 4. Refunds

If you were charged incorrectly (for example, charged twice), we refund the difference to the original payment method within **5 business days** of confirming the error. Refunds for deposits follow the Cancellations and Rescheduling policy.

We do not refund work that was completed as quoted. If you are unhappy with the work itself, contact us first: we will come back and fix it under our warranty.

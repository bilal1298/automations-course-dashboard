# Cancellations and Rescheduling

- **Doc ID:** BG-POL-03
- **Version:** 2
- **Effective:** 1 January 2026

## 1. Rescheduling

You can reschedule a booking for free if you tell us at least **24 hours** before the booked time. Reply to your booking confirmation, text the office number or call us.

## 2. Late cancellations

If you cancel with less than 24 hours' notice, we charge a late cancellation fee of **$60**. If our electrician arrives and can't get access (nobody home, locked gate, dog loose), we charge the normal call-out fee.

## 3. Deposits

Jobs over $3,000 need a 20% deposit to book. Deposits are refunded in full if you cancel more than 7 days before the start date. Within 7 days, we keep the cost of any parts we have already ordered for your job and refund the rest.

## 4. If we cancel

If we have to cancel or run more than 2 hours late, we will contact you as early as possible and you will not be charged any fee. If we miss a booking without telling you, your next call-out fee is free.

# Warranty Policy

- **Doc ID:** BG-POL-02
- **Version:** 2
- **Effective:** 1 July 2026
- **Replaces:** version 1 (1 March 2024) for all jobs completed on or after 1 July 2026

## 1. Workmanship warranty

We guarantee our workmanship for **24 months** from the date the job is completed. If something we installed fails because of how we installed it, we come back and fix it at no charge, including the call-out fee.

## 2. Parts

Parts we supply carry the manufacturer's warranty. LED downlights and switchboard components we supply are covered for 5 years by the manufacturer. We handle the claim with the supplier for you for the full manufacturer warranty period.

## 3. What is not covered

- Customer-supplied parts (for example, a ceiling fan you bought yourself). We still warrant our installation of them for 24 months.
- Damage from storms, power surges, pests or water.
- Work changed by someone else after we finished.

## 4. How to make a claim

Email warranty@brightgully.example with your job number and a photo if you can, or call the office. We aim to respond within 1 business day, and the same day for anything unsafe.

## 5. What changed from version 1

Workmanship warranty went from 12 to 24 months, the call-out fee is now explicitly free for warranty visits, and installation of customer-supplied parts is now covered.

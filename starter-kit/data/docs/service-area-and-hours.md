# Service Area and Business Hours

- **Doc ID:** BG-POL-04
- **Version:** 1
- **Effective:** 1 March 2024

## 1. Where we work

We work on Brisbane's northside, within about 25 km of our base in Chermside. That includes suburbs such as Aspley, Albany Creek, Bald Hills, Bridgeman Downs, Carseldine, Everton Park, Geebung, Kedron, Lutwyche, McDowall, Nundah, Stafford, Strathpine, Wavell Heights and Zillmere.

We do not travel to the Gold Coast, Sunshine Coast, Ipswich or Toowoomba. For jobs outside our area we are happy to recommend another licensed electrician.

## 2. Office hours

The office answers phones and emails Monday to Friday, 7am to 5pm. Messages left outside these hours are answered the next business day, except emergencies (see the Emergency and Safety Policy).

## 3. Work hours

Normal jobs: Monday to Friday, 7am to 5pm. Saturday and after-hours work (weekdays 5pm to 10pm) is available at after-hours rates. Commercial clients can book Sunday work at Sunday rates.

## 4. What we do and don't do

We do residential and light commercial electrical work: lighting, power points, switchboards, smoke alarms, EV chargers, ceiling fans and fault finding.

We do **not** do solar panel or battery installation, air-conditioning installation, data/network cabling, plumbing or gas.

## 1. LED downlights

The 10 W tri-colour LED downlights we stock (90 mm cut-out) can switch between warm white, neutral and cool white with a switch on the driver. They are dimmable with most trailing-edge dimmers.

## 2. Smoke alarms

From 1 January 2027, every Queensland home must have interconnected photoelectric smoke alarms in every bedroom, in hallways connecting bedrooms, and on every level. Rentals and homes being sold already must comply.

## 4. EV chargers

The 7 kW single-phase wall chargers we install need a dedicated 40 A circuit. Three-phase 22 kW chargers need a three-phase supply, which most Brisbane homes don't have.

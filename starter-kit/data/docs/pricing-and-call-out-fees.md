# Pricing and Call-Out Fees

- **Doc ID:** BG-POL-01
- **Version:** 3
- **Effective:** 1 July 2026
- **Owner:** Office manager

Bright Gully Electrical is a fictional business used for practice. All prices include GST.

## 1. How we charge

We charge a call-out fee for every visit, plus labour in 15-minute blocks after the first 30 minutes, plus parts. The call-out fee covers travel and the first 30 minutes on site.

We give fixed-price quotes for jobs we can see in advance, such as switchboard upgrades, EV chargers and renovations. Quotes are valid for 30 days.

## 2. Rates

| Service                         | Weekday 7am–5pm | After hours / Saturday | Sunday & public holidays |
|---------------------------------|-----------------|------------------------|--------------------------|
| Call-out fee (incl. first 30 min) | $99           | $180                   | $250                     |
| Labour per extra 15 min         | $28             | $45                    | $60                      |
| Emergency call-out (2 hr response) | n/a          | $280                   | $350                     |
| Smoke alarm compliance check (per property) | $149 | not offered           | not offered              |

"After hours" means weekdays 5pm–10pm. Between 10pm and 7am only emergency call-outs are available.

## 3. Common fixed prices

- Replace a standard light switch or power point: from $139 (includes call-out).
- Install a customer-supplied ceiling fan on an existing light point: $189 per fan.
- 7 kW EV charger install, switchboard within 10 m: from $1,250 (charger not included).
- Switchboard upgrade with safety switches (single phase): from $1,950.

## 4. Things we don't charge for

- Quotes for jobs over $1,000.
- Going back to fix our own workmanship under warranty (see the Warranty Policy).

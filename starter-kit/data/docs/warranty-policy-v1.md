# Warranty Policy

- **Doc ID:** BG-POL-02
- **Version:** 1
- **Effective:** 1 March 2024
- **Status:** SUPERSEDED by version 2 on 1 July 2026. Kept for jobs completed before that date.

## 1. Workmanship warranty

We guarantee our workmanship for **12 months** from the date the job is completed. If something we installed fails because of how we installed it, we come back and fix it at no charge.

## 2. Parts

Parts we supply carry the manufacturer's warranty, usually 12 months. We handle the claim with the supplier for you during the first 12 months.

## 3. What is not covered

- Customer-supplied parts (for example, a ceiling fan you bought yourself).
- Damage from storms, power surges, pests or water.
- Work changed by someone else after we finished.

## 4. How to make a claim

Email office@brightgully.example with your job number and a photo if you can. We aim to respond within 2 business days.

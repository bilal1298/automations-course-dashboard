# Emergency and Safety Policy

- **Doc ID:** BG-POL-06
- **Version:** 2
- **Effective:** 1 January 2026

## 1. What counts as an emergency

- A burning smell, smoke, sparks or scorch marks from a switchboard, power point or appliance.
- Loss of power to all or part of the property that is not a network outage.
- A safety switch (RCD) that trips and will not reset.
- Exposed live wires, for example after storm damage.

A flickering light or a single dead power point is usually **not** an emergency and is booked as a normal job.

## 2. What to do first

If there is smoke, fire or someone has had an electric shock, call **000** first. If it is safe to do so, switch off the main switch at the switchboard. Do not touch anything that is sparking or hot.

If the whole street is out, check the Energex outage map first: that is a network fault, not something we can fix.

## 3. Our emergency service

Emergency call-outs are available 24/7 within our service area. We aim to be on site within **2 hours**. Emergency rates are in the Pricing and Call-Out Fees policy.

## 4. Who handles emergency messages

Any message mentioning burning, smoke, sparks or no power must be passed to the on-call electrician immediately by phone. It must never wait in an inbox or a review queue.
